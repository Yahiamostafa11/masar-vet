Hero background clips: hero-1.mp4 ... hero-6.mp4 (played in order, cross-fading). jpg files are poster frames (unused by the site, safe to delete).
To use different clips, edit the paths in scripts/videos.mjs and run: node scripts/videos.mjs
(1080p, 30fps, no audio, max 10 s each, ~1-2 MB per clip). Add/remove clips by editing CLIPS in client/src/components/HeroBackdrop.jsx.
Visitors with reduced-motion or data-saver never download the videos.
